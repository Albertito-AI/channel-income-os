# Commercial licensing and commissioned reviews

Channel Income OS is publicly inspectable. Its public licence permits
noncommercial purposes. A revenue-generating creator, agency, consultant,
brand, software service or other commercial operator needs a separate written
commercial licence before commercial use.

## Scope of an engagement

A licence enquiry can address internal use for a named creator, an agency's
client-channel analysis, or an institutional workflow. The written agreement
identifies the licensed version, permitted users and channels, duration and
support. Redistribution, resale, white-labelling and hosted service rights
require express agreement.

A commissioned channel review may examine one authorised set of Studio exports
and produce a reviewed report, prioritised action queue and implementation note.
The agreed scope determines the required data, deliverables and review process.
Google account access is not required.

## Important limits

A licence is permission to use software. It is not a warranty of platform access,
monetisation status, audience growth, advertising revenue or any other commercial
result. The operator remains responsible for platform compliance, content rights,
consumer law, tax and financial reporting.

This page describes enquiry routes as of 9 November 2026. It does not alter an
existing signed licence or agreement.
